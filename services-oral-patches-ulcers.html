Understanding your care

About white patches, red patches & ulcers

White or red patches and ulcers can have different causes, from irritation to conditions needing further investigation. A clinical assessment is important when a change persists or repeatedly returns.

The dentist reviews the area, symptom history and potential triggers. They may recommend monitoring, treatment, a referral or additional testing depending on the findings.

What to expect from your visit

  • An examination and discussion of your concerns
  • A personalised plan with options explained
  • Guidance on treatment, aftercare and review

This is general information, not a diagnosis. Suitability, risks, costs and expected outcomes are discussed after an individual assessment.

When to speak with your dentist

A mouth ulcer or patch lasting more than two weeks, or a recurring unexplained change.
