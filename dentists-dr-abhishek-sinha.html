Dr. Abhishek Sinha

BDS, MDS · Dentistry, Lucknow

Your dental team

Care with attention to the individual.

Dr. Abhishek Sinha holds BDS and MDS qualifications and is part of the dental team at Dentistry, a multispeciality dental clinic and implant center in Lucknow.

Born in Lucknow, Dr. Abhishek Sinha completed his MDS in Oral Medicine and Radiology at Saveetha Dental College, Chennai, under Dr. MGR Medical University, Tamil Nadu, in 2007. His dissertation examined the role of C-reactive protein in periodontal diseases.

His clinic profile describes him as a professor and head of Oral Medicine and Radiology at Sardar Patel Post Graduate Institute of Dental and Medical Sciences, Lucknow, with more than 15 years of undergraduate and postgraduate academic experience. It also describes him as a trained and certified implant dentist with national and international publications.

His areas of interest include oral mucosal abnormalities, oral cancer, salivary gland disorders and facial pain. The clinic highlights his care for children and patients whose medical conditions require additional treatment considerations, together with his emphasis on explaining problems and procedures clearly.

At your visit, the dentist will listen to your concerns, review relevant health information and explain the findings. Treatment recommendations are based on an individual examination, with options and next steps discussed clearly.
